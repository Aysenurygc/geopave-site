§FOR MANUFACTURERSTürkiye

Technical representation in Türkiye for road-technology manufacturers.

Geopave Mühendislik is a pavement engineering consultancy in Ankara. Alongside its engineering work it represents road-technology manufacturers in Türkiye: survey systems, asphalt chemistry, laboratory equipment and repair technologies. If you make products for road pavements and are looking for a Turkish partner, this page is for you.

§ 01THE OFFER3 points

Your product, presented by engineers.

Road authorities and contractors in Türkiye buy against specifications and tender files. We sell the way they buy: by connecting a product to the specification, the application conditions and the result.

01

Engineers in front of the buyer

Technical questions are answered by civil engineers who work on pavement performance, not by a general sales team.

02

Inside an engineering practice

Geopave also delivers pavement engineering, quality control and R&D work. A product is linked from the services in which it is used, and its page links back to them.

03

A Turkish catalogue that is open

Products are presented on Turkish catalogue pages. Datasheets and safety data sheets will be downloadable there without a form, so buyers can write your product into a specification.

§ 02MARKET AND PORTFOLIOTable 1

Who buys road technology in Türkiye.

  • The General Directorate of Highways (Karayolları Genel Müdürlüğü) and other road authorities
  • Municipalities
  • Contractors and asphalt plants
  • Testing laboratories and universities

These are the buyer groups Geopave's products and services address. Volumes and named accounts are not published here.
